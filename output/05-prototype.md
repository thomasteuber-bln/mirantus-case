# 05 — Prototype

Flow A, one clickable flow, built from `output/04-prototype-brief.md` and `data/constraints.md`.

Link: https://claude.ai/artifact/BZ72zi9K348i7ZeeAiVru6 

Screens: ophthalmologist interval field → "Ihr Ergebnis ist da" e-mail (consent re-ask if no consent) → report box → reminder e-mail at due date → booking at the optician.

How to use it:
- The "Einwilligung bei Buchung" toggle switches between a customer who consented at booking and one who did not.
- If there is no consent and the re-ask box is left unticked, or if the ophthalmologist picks "keine Angabe", the flow ends after the report and no reminder is shown.
- All names, dates and addresses are placeholders in [brackets].
- The back-catalogue copy variant from the brief is not built as a screen.
